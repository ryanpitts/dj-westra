DJ Erik Westra

  • ↬ Hit me up Minneapolis
  • Erik Westra has been playing records around Minneapolis (Steady Pour, Berlin, Icehouse, etc.) and Chicago (Whistler, Empty Bottle, Danny's, etc.) for decades, spinning unique and vibey records from his obsessively large vinyl collection. His sets range from psych and garage rock, rare soul and funk, '80s synth-pop and new wave to '90s hip-hop, weird arty stuff, dollar-bin gems, and everything in between.
